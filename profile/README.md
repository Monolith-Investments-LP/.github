Monolith Investments is an infrastructure and macro investor. Artificial intelligence is discussed as software, but it arrives as concrete, copper, turbines, and land — a capital cycle with a physical bill, paid years before the returns are legible. We were founded by [Arya Somu](https://aryasomu.com/) in 2026 and are headquartered in San Francisco.

Our work centers on:

1. Concentrated positions across the physical and financial layers of the buildout, sized by conviction rather than spread thin for its own sake
2. A discretionary macro book, because financing a buildout of this size is a story about rates, currencies, and sovereign appetite — power is a commodity story, and fabrication capacity is a geopolitical one
3. Interconnection as the binding constraint: utilities, regulators, supply chains, and whoever already owns the site resolve on schedules that quarterly attention spans do not reward
4. Building our own research and execution stack instead of renting one — operating inside a buildout is the cheapest way to learn where its constraints actually bind.

We are long-term oriented, patient with capital cycles that resolve slowly, and alert to the difference between a thesis being right and being right on time.
